## Spec change — film length, scene length, paywall (later same day)

After designing the player around the original ≤ 3 min film / 15 s scene
spec, we walked through the math on a few candidate film lengths and
free-content wedges. Settled on a single locked v1 spec:

- **Micro film:** ~15 minutes total runtime (was: ≤ 3 min).
- **Scene:** 45–90 seconds, default 90 s (was: locked at 15 s).
- **Free wedge:** first **3 scenes** free.
- **Pass:** $2.49 unlocks every scene of one micro film.
- **All-access:** $9.99/mo unlocks every scene of every film.

We also deleted "episode" and "series" from the product vocabulary. We
make films, not shows. The watch page used to be modeled as a series of
short micro film episodes (ReelShort-style); it is now modeled as a
single film whose scenes are progressively unlocked. Same UI affordance,
different framing — it is one film, not a show.

### Decisions

- A scene is a 45–90 s render. No upper-bound shorter than 45 s and no
  upper-bound longer than 90 s in v1. Creators choose where in the range
  each scene falls.
- A standard micro film is ~15 minutes total. Concrete scene counts in
  sample data range from 11 to 14 to model real creator variability.
- The free wedge is exactly 3 scenes. Not 2, not 4, not "first ~20%".
  Three is the rule.
- Pricing is locked at $2.49 / Pass and $9.99 / mo All-access for v1.
- Pricing strings live as constants in `lib/workflow.ts` and are read
  by every surface that displays them. No hardcoded prices in JSX.

## Cap micro films at 10 minutes (later same day)

Tightened the form spec. A micro film is now **capped at 10 minutes**.
Films can run much shorter — a single 90-second beat, a tight three-
minute essay, a full-length nine-minute thriller all qualify — but
nothing exceeds the cap. This is the right cinema-of-the-phone
runtime: long enough to hold a real arc, short enough that a viewer
will actually finish.

### Files updated

- `lib/workflow.ts` — `FILM_TOTAL_DURATION` removed; replaced with
  `FILM_MAX_DURATION` (`"10 min"`, the cap value) and
  `FILM_MAX_DURATION_LABEL` (`"Up to 10 min"`, the UX label that
  signals not every film hits the cap). Doc comment, "Stitch" step
  copy, Brands and Filmmakers use cases, "What Is a Micro Film?" blog
  post, and the testimonial all reworded. `filmConcepts` now carries
  per-concept runtimes (8/9/9 min) and scene counts (8/9/7) instead of
  reusing one constant for all three. `SCENE_DURATION` legacy alias
  retained.
- `lib/films.ts` — showcase film "The Window Across the Way"
  re-curated from 12 scenes (~13 min, would have exceeded the cap)
  down to 8 scenes (~9m 55s). Kept the strongest beats: setup →
  pattern → corroboration → escalation → turning point → confrontation
  → twist → climax. `totalDuration` now reads "9 min 55 sec." Plot
  reworded ("Eight micro scenes. Just under ten minutes.").
- `app/page.tsx` — hero subtitle ("A handful of micro scenes. Up to
  ten minutes."), Film stat, hero card scene counter ("01 / 08"),
  concepts section header, and showcase callout all retuned.
- `app/studio/page.tsx` — metadata, hero copy, and slate panel all
  retuned to use `FILM_MAX_DURATION_LABEL`.
- `app/watch/[slug]/page.tsx` — scene grid widened from
  `lg:grid-cols-6` to `sm:grid-cols-8` so the 8 scenes lay out as a
  clean single film-strip row at sm+. Scene count text is
  data-driven, so it auto-updates to "8 micro scenes · 3 out · 5
  coming soon."
- `app/api/runs/route.ts` — `FILM_TARGET_MINUTES = 15` →
  `FILM_MAX_MINUTES = 10`; response field renamed `filmTargetMinutes`
  → `filmMaxMinutes`.
- `app/layout.tsx` — root metadata description updated to "up to ten
  minutes."
- `docs/style-guide.md`, `docs/README.md`, `README.md` — vocab and
  pipeline definitions updated to reflect the cap.

### Decisions

- **The cap is 10 minutes, not 9, not 12.** Cleaner verbal handle
  ("up to ten minutes"), maps to the upper bound of what people
  actually finish on a phone, and gives a comfortable headroom over
  the showcase's ~10-min thriller without bloating the form.
- **Films can be much shorter, no floor.** A single 90-second micro
  scene is a valid micro film. The pipeline should not enforce a
  minimum.
- **Constant naming reflects the semantics.** `FILM_MAX_DURATION` (for
  the cap) and `FILM_MAX_DURATION_LABEL` (for the "Up to" UX label).
  Don't bring back `FILM_TOTAL_DURATION` — it implied every film *is*
  that long, which is now wrong.
- **Showcase film resized to 8 scenes, not 7 or 9.** Eight beats
  carries a complete thriller arc and lays out as a single neat row
  on the scene grid (`sm:grid-cols-8`).
